# The tribute experience

The detailed page goals, destinations and return paths are in [EXPERIENCE_BLUEPRINT.md](EXPERIENCE_BLUEPRINT.md). That document is the navigation skeleton for this implementation.

## A person, then a meaningful moment

Meet Swamiji through the persistent rotating portraits. Neighbouring photographs remain visible and the palette changes with the selected side of his life. The visitor may pause, tap, swipe or use the keyboard.

His Life in Action leads into generous editorial scenes, derived from the existing Traditional Events presentation. A photograph, a short heading, context and one clear action introduce each occasion. A secondary preview reveals the next story. Occasions are not presented as a landing-page grid or folder catalogue.

Inside an occasion, preserve image proportions, place the photograph or recording in a focused stage, and keep captions and return/next paths available. A small moment ribbon is a direct control inside the selected occasion, never the primary way to discover Swamiji.

## One teaching gateway

Learn Swamiji’s Teachings presents four distinct invitations: Satsangh Messages, Books, Message Cards & Blessings, and Everyday Teachings.

- Satsangh and Everyday Teachings present one paper reflection in the foreground with neighbouring pages behind. Search, month and topic tools open only when requested. Finding a thought returns the visitor to the same staged experience with matching reflections, rather than to rows of results.
- Books presents a featured physical cover with neighbouring actual covers on the shelf. Pearls opens its dedicated welcome and reading room, preserving the cover opening, page turns, saved place, bookmarks, contents, search and appearance controls.
- Message Cards preserves the existing rotating spotlight and folded-card reader. Outside/inside sheet mapping, original page sequence, enlargement, keyboard/swipe and original PDFs stay intact.
- Longer dated reflections and practical teachings open intentional readers with source distinctions and an onward reading route. Their text may scroll for legibility.

## Service first

The Foundation introduces purpose, actual service and ways to connect. Historical support uses a composed service story. Reviewed acknowledgement images are supporting material opened on request. They are not the cover of a receipt dashboard. Where no occasion photograph was supplied, use an identified portrait of Swamiji or a supplied view of the place; do not imply a photograph of another visit documents that support.

## Appearance and comfort

Use real photographs, restrained serif headings, clear everyday captions, light contextual palettes and quiet green media/reading spaces. Give the image room. Keep the native pointer, clear focus and readable contrast. No decorative replacement icons for missing photographs. No long scrolling homepage, boxed archive landings or folder-tree navigation.

Automatic movement is confined to introductory portrait/card scenes, with pause and reduced-motion support. Reading and playing media remain under the visitor’s control.

## Content and publication

Keep category/event/type directories behind the scenes for maintainers. Canonical content and future intake are documented separately. Music remains unpublished until real material arrives. Seven commercial books retain supplied publication destinations; they do not imply full digital texts.

The previous website remains independent. This project is a local reviewable repository; a build does not publish it remotely.
