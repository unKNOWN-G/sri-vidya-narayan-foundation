# Refreshed content audit

The source website was reviewed in Chrome Incognito before this rebuild. The legacy site remains intact.

The project contains 40 organized event/service collections after merging overlapping event references, 132 edited daily readings, 180 Pearls of Wisdom, 38 practical teachings, eight books, eight message collections and twelve giving records. Counts are a captured import, not an assertion that later uploads are already included.

The source continues to change across other chats. The full timestamped original catalog and original media are retained locally under media/ and are excluded from Git/public builds.

## Editorial decisions

- Guru Purnima event photographs, Nada Neerajanam music photographs and Varamahalakshmi prototype event cards used sample media; they were not imported as real events. Authentic message documents with Guru Purnima titles remain available.
- The user confirmed music/Veena photographs have not yet been provided. The music category is an unpublished draft with an intake folder.
- The 2022 C.K. Palya vision camp and report, Dhanvantari Day photographs, and Yelahanka school visit are now discoverable even though earlier compiled legacy pages did not surface all of them.
- The dates of undated events are not inferred from download filenames.
- Gollapalli birthday media are not presented as photographs of the 2004 school opening.
- Corpus principal, annual interest and repeated acknowledgements are not added together. Donor names are retained for the two Yelahanka contributions.
- Clinical, school and financial evidence uses only previously published/redacted material. Raw personal documents and private scan sources remain out of the public build.
- Original message spreads and text reading panels are distinct: two original spread images are not relabelled as four separate photographs.

The final source delta also includes the 78th birthday gathering, farmers at Annavaram, 50th Chaturmasya Puja and Radha Kalyanam. Veda Sabha retains its two supplied YouTube recordings.
