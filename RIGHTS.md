# Rights and source material

This project does not grant a blanket license to its photographs, recordings, documents, book covers, artwork, or source code. No MIT or other open-source license is implied by putting the project in Git.

- Supplied photographs, video, audio, manuscripts, and documents retain their existing ownership and terms. Keep their provenance in the local media catalog.
- Book covers and publisher/store artwork identify the relevant publications. Their inclusion does not transfer their rights or imply that book sales fund the foundation.
- Preserve the license and attribution files accompanying fonts or other third-party components. The website's build does not change those terms.
- The project owner can choose an appropriate source-code license separately. That choice should identify which files it covers and exclude third-party material where necessary.

For each newly added collection, record its source and any reuse conditions with the event notes. Material that is not ready for public delivery belongs in the local archive or intake area rather than under `assets/`.
